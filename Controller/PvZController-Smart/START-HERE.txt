PvZ SMART CONTROLLER

1. Start Plants vs. Zombies.
2. Open PvZController.exe.
3. Click Connect.
4. Keep "Auto-choose plants and press Let's Rock" checked.
5. Enter Adventure or another normal level.
6. At Choose Your Plants, click Start in the controller.

The bot selects a scene-appropriate loadout, presses Let's Rock, then plants
through the real seed cards. PvZ therefore applies normal sun costs and card
cooldowns. It reads live zombies and plants to choose a row and tile, and it
re-evaluates the board after plants are eaten.

Auto-collect is enabled when the controller connects, and the controller keeps
sun at 5,000,000 whenever a board is loaded.

Leave "Free planting" OFF for normal cooldown and sun behavior.

Supported game build:
English Origin GOTY 1.2.0.1073

Emergency Stop stops the bot, restores controller-owned patches, and detaches.
